Devices
Paired devices talk over an encrypted connection that only accepts the exact devices you paired. Turning this on opens network port 41801; Windows may ask once whether to allow BE-Aero through its firewall.
On the device that shows a code, press "Show pairing code". On the other one, pick it from the list (or type its address) and enter the code. You confirm on both.
Only the theme is kept the same unless you turn more on. Web widgets are shared one at a time: right-click one to share it with all your devices or send it to one. Window state (size, position, minimized) always stays per device.
